## Polyhedral invariants

The Klein relative invariants, from which the polyhedral ornament family is built. A ratio of these
carries the full rotation symmetry of a Platonic solid **only at equal binary degree**, where the
automorphy factors cancel — a ratio of unequal degree renders and is not symmetric.

Transcribing these is the risk they exist to remove: the forms in the literature cohere as a set only
for one orientation, and the commonly-remembered signs mix orientations, which silently destroys
rotation invariance. Klein's syzygy is the check that catches it.
